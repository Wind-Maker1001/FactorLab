# Cash-funded portfolio execution

A complete weight vector is an immutable rebalance input. Weights are never normalized or redistributed after an execution failure. Sizing uses official NAV and explicit settlement cash, not margin buying power. Economic exposure multipliers affect reporting, not sizing; a leveraged ETF is still bought at its nominal target weight.

## Interfaces and ownership

`PortfolioExecutor` accepts the shared `TradingAdapter` operations, with `OrderAbsent` representing an authoritative original-client-ID lookup miss. A miss after an attempted POST does not authorize resubmission: a broker may still be eventually consistent. The adapter must return incremental fill prices with cumulative filled quantities, as required by `OrderEvent`.

`create` records account, strategy, version, trading session, complete weights, owned instruments and explicitly adopted initial inventory. The account/strategy/session/signal identity is immutable, including across version changes. By default the signal identity is the session; an explicit stable `rebalance_id` permits distinct intraday decisions without weakening duplicate-signal protection. All owners of an account must share one `PortfolioStore`; per-instrument claims prevent another strategy from adopting its inventory. An observed account position is not evidence of ownership. External holdings and orders are retained; discrepancies block the affected instrument.

`run_once` performs one event-driven phase without waiting for fills. Call it again after an order/account update or a scheduled reconciliation. Reduction orders must be confirmed terminal before increases are planned. Each child intent is committed before POST, and a separate committed submission marker survives a process exit during POST. Recovery queries original client identities account-wide, including canceled predecessors. A committed submission marker may resume a first POST only when `SubmissionNotAttempted` supplies durable adapter-journal evidence that the HTTP submission was never entered; a REST 404 is insufficient. Otherwise the order remains uncertain. An uncertain submitted order blocks further risk until its official state is recovered. A cancellation is not permission to submit a replacement over unconfirmed fills.

`ExecutionQuote` requires symbol identity, bid/ask, UTC event/receipt times, session and source. Signal-dependent callers supply `not_before`: an execution quote must be later than the strategy input observation. Missing or stale quotes affect dependent instruments only. `InstrumentRules` supplies quantity step, minimum reasonable notional, tradability and TIF. Limit buys are cash-funded at the ask; limit sells are bounded by strategy inventory.

The planner extends the foundation in PR #239 with explicit settlement currency while preserving its default USDT behavior and reduction-first API. `TradingController` retains its existing local-paper boundary. Broker networking and market-data acquisition are separate integrations.

## Shared capital execution boundary

Alpaca instance hosting depends on `trading/capital.py` for static allocation, exclusive ownership, qualification and fee-aware reservation admission. `portfolio_execution.py` owns the common outbox, terminal/uncertain-order recovery and expiry; `portfolio_accounting.py`, `control.py` and `paper.py` preserve its accounting and shared exposure-limit semantics. These are shared execution dependencies, not a second strategy-management implementation.

`capital_adoption.py` and `capital_migration.py` implement the executor's existing-journal adoption APIs, preserving original cash contributions, orders, events and corporate actions. `capital_funding.py` supplies the shared policy's Binance funding attribution; Alpaca does not use funding adjustments. Keep these modules with their public executor/policy callers so both integrations consume one complete implementation. ## Static instance capital policy

`PortfolioExecutor.capital_policy.configure(account_id=..., currency=..., budget=..., fee_rate=...)` explicitly enables static account budgeting and exclusive symbol admission. Configuration is immutable and does not grant exchange authorization. The fee reservation rate is an explicit upper bound supplied with the account policy; it is not inferred from observed fills.

Register initial cash and explicitly owned inventory through `PortfolioExecutor.allocate`, then bind the stable execution owner through `capital_policy.bind`. At the V2 boundary, `binding.instance_id` is that owner; `binding.strategy_id` identifies the logical strategy and is retained separately. `capital_policy.qualify(spec, binding)` validates declared demand against the existing allocation and reserves exclusive symbols atomically. Qualification does not allocate funds. Pausing an instance retains its capital and claims and does not pause another owner. Original-order reconciliation continues while paused, so authoritative terminal events can release reservations after restart without admitting new submissions.

Direct orders reserve cash in the same transaction that inserts their original identities into the shared outbox. An oversized request rolls back its claims, plan and orders. Unknown submission outcomes retain reservations until authoritative reconciliation. `plan_identity` exposes the existing account/owner/session/signal hash for receipt replay without regenerating decisions from changed positions.

`reconcile(account_id)` transactionally imports original-order observations without creating plans, submitting orders or canceling them. Paused hosts may call it to persist cumulative fills and fees and release authoritative terminal reservations. It writes the execution journal and is distinct from the read-only risk snapshot; it does not expire or abandon plans.

`expire(identity)` durably freezes a plan before canceling its original children. It returns `WAITING` while any result remains unknown and `COMPLETE` only when all children have authoritative terminal outcomes. Only `SubmissionNotAttempted` permits a local unsubmitted cancellation; an exchange lookup miss does not release the reservation. Repeated expiration resumes cleanup, preserves `EXPIRED_REBALANCE`, never regenerates child IDs, and leaves ownership and confirmed fills intact. `abandon` reuses this path with `ABANDONED_REBALANCE` and requires completed cleanup.

`capital_policy.snapshot` returns allocated capital, cash, outstanding reservations and pause state. `risk_snapshot(executor, account_id, official_equity=...)` reads a single official account observation and the shared journal without recovering orders or saving reports. The cash-funded snapshot reuses `build_portfolio_report` for basis, fees and realized/unrealized PnL. For Binance USD-M, the adapter supplies `margin_facts()`, `funding_events()` and `funding_coverage()`. Occupied margin comes from official position initial margin, not purchase notional. Confirmed funding is attributed against historical initial inventory and cumulative fills, never the current symbol owner; ambiguous same-timestamp fills, missing coverage or unknown owners leave funding/NAV unknown and block additional funded risk. Known funding debits tighten admission; credits do not expand static authorization. Actual exchange available balance and the existing account controller remain authoritative.

`capital_policy.set_limits(account_id, owner, RiskLimits(...))` optionally installs immutable tighter gross caps. Defaults are the allocated capital. Execution reuses the controller's `exposure_limit_reason` for order, instrument and total exposure caps; unfilled buys remain gross risk until confirmed terminal. `capital_policy.propose(account_id, optimizer, OptimizationInput(...))` passes owner-labeled risk data and current static weights through `PortfolioOptimizer.optimize`; the returned recommendation never changes allocations.

### Existing execution journals

`adopt_allocation(account_id=..., strategy_id=..., allocation_id=..., capital=..., current_cash=..., official_equity=..., receipt_id=...)` attaches an allocation to an existing cash-funded owner. Supply confirmed current cash and current cash-plus-marked-inventory capital. The initial cash contribution is reconstructed by subtracting the existing journal's net cash flow; passing current cash to `allocate` would debit historical fills twice and is rejected. Original orders and fills are unchanged. An identical receipt is idempotent; changing its financial inputs is rejected.

`adopt_existing(receipt_id=..., account_id=..., sleeves=..., official_equity=...)` atomically imports explicitly identified original journals into a configured account. Each sleeve supplies `instance_id`, `logical_strategy`, `allocation_id`, `capital`, `initial_cash`, `initial_inventory={symbol: (quantity, entry_price)}`, `owned_symbols`, `created_at`, `trading_session`, original outbox `orders`, original `OrderEvent` entries in `events`, and `source_receipt`. The receipt must declare `history_complete=True` and the complete original `order_ids` and `event_ids`. Original cumulative quantities, notionals and fees must be reproduced by those confirmations. When the source journal contains corporate actions, include their unchanged payloads in `corporate_actions` and their complete IDs in `source_receipt.corporate_action_ids`; quantity transformations, basis and cash postings are replayed and retained together. Pending orders retain their identities and remaining reservations; completed plans cannot regenerate historical orders. Binance migration additionally requires complete original funding coverage. Migration never synthesizes missing history or adjusts initial cash to conceal a reconciliation difference.

## Accounting

`build_portfolio_report` consumes persisted original intents and cumulative confirmations, official account holdings, official equity and separately identified research adjustments. It reconstructs owned weighted-average cost and realized/unrealized PnL without attributing external inventory to the strategy. Official equity/cash remain unmodified. Research dividends or costs absent from the broker are reflected only in economic NAV; settling a receivable into official cash is not a second income event. Gross turnover is buy plus sell notional divided by final official equity. Slippage is signed execution cost relative to the recorded child-order quote, before legal tick rounding. Weight tracking error is the Euclidean distance between target and actual security weights plus the cash-weight component, not a time-series return tracking-error estimate. Initial capital may be supplied explicitly; otherwise the first observed official NAV is retained as the funding baseline.

## Original v69 order semantics

Original v69 generates its weekly orders from the previous completed session's adjusted close and volume; observing the current opening is not a signal prerequisite. The target-weight vector is not sufficient to reconstruct those orders at current execution prices. Preserve the original 0.005 weight-change deadband, zero-target liquidation branch, and quantity cap of 0.001 times previous-session adjusted volume, including the cap on liquidation orders. Adjusted research shares must be converted to real broker shares using the matching adjustment ratio; execution constraints may reduce an order but must not regenerate its size from current-price target weights.

`adjusted_order_deltas` translates the native strategy's already-generated orders into signed real shares. `PortfolioExecutor.create(quantity_deltas=..., signal_inputs=...)` persists those original deltas and their provenance, preserves the signed requested quantities independently of predecessor late fills, and bypasses target-weight sizing. Remaining quantities deduct only this plan's confirmed fills; recovered predecessor fills cannot reverse a native order's direction. Broker quantity-step rounding and cash constraints only reduce the requested order. Focused validation including explicit-quantity execution passed 54 tests. `create_strategy_plan` accepts the native decision dictionary and commits the original orders, strategy checkpoint, ratios and caller-supplied history identity in the same immutable plan. A suppressed weekly decision creates no new plan. Nine native-strategy differential cases passed against the frozen originals, covering deadband, zero-target exit, buy/sell volume caps, previous-session preparation and weekly restart; the public migration bridge from `313a79a` is integrated. Its `create_alpaca_plan` passes matching factors, broker steps, signed quantities and strategy checkpoint directly through `create`, with immutable replay validation.
